# Why Tally works the way it does

Volunteering should be fun and easy, for the volunteer and for the coordinator.

Volunteers need a lot of hands-on direction. Right now all of it comes from one coordinator, so volunteers wait for her, hunt for her, or stand around. Tally puts that direction on the volunteer's phone and gives the coordinator their day back.

## What the research told us

Full sources are in Emily's research (GitHub issue #1).

**Volunteers**
- Standing around with nothing to do is the most common complaint.
- A first-timer decides on day one whether they'll come back.
- People who report something heavy almost never hear what happened.
- Most people who stopped volunteering would come back if someone asked.
- Volunteers would rather get a thank-you by name than a gift.
- In one survey of 7,215 volunteers, 46% were over 55. So no download, no password, big type.
- Students, court-ordered volunteers and employer matching all need proof of hours.

**Coordinators**
- Only about 1 in 5 volunteer managers works on volunteers full time. 37% had no training in the past year.
- They answer the same questions all day and type paper sign-in sheets at night.
- Hours get counted twice or never, then rebuilt from five tools at grant time.
- Audits fail on three things: no same-day record, double counts, no supervisor sign-off.
- Worrying things volunteers notice get lost on paper or in a group chat.
- One volunteer hour is worth $34.79 to a nonprofit, and that number goes in grant applications.

## Every screen, and the problem it answers

**Volunteer side**

| Screen | Problem it fixes |
|---|---|
| Home: "Do this next" card | Nobody hunts for a coordinator. The card changes as the shift moves: find your host, grab a task, wrap up. |
| Event card: parking, what to wear, where bags go | First-timers arrive not knowing where to go. |
| I'm here | Starts the shift clock. First-timers get a badge on the staff page so someone thanks them before they leave. |
| I'm free and Tasks | Standing around. Tally hands out the next task out loud. |
| Ask | The coordinator answers the same questions all day. Answers come only from the host's notes. If the notes don't say, Tally names who to ask. It never makes one up. |
| Chat with a sun for "Everyone today" and a host icon | One place for the host and the crew. Staff can see team chat, and it reminds people not to share client names. |
| Report | Three levels. Danger shows 911 on the volunteer's own screen. Complaints can stay private. The volunteer hears back when staff mark it handled. |
| Talk | Say your hours in English, Spanish or both. Counted once, even if you say it twice. Thanked by name, out loud. |
| Hours letter | Proof for school, court or work, with a code anyone can check. |

**Coordinator side**

| Screen | Problem it fixes |
|---|---|
| "10 volunteers gave 55 hours this week" | The week in one line, plus a lime link to the hours waiting for their OK. |
| Needs attention | Urgent first. Mark handled shows a check and tells you the volunteer will see it. |
| Tasks box | Post tasks once. Tally hands them out when volunteers tap I'm free. |
| Approve in one tap | Same-day record plus supervisor sign-off, which covers the top audit failures. Each approved shift gets a proof code. |
| Grant report | One download. No rebuilding hours from paper. |

## Changed on Nov 16

- Volunteer Home page: the logo and the Shift tab did the same thing. Home now leads with one "do this next" card, then host, goal, your hours and the latest team update, then shortcuts to Ask, Tasks, Report and the hours letter. The old Shift tab is now Talk.
- Walkthrough runs on both sides and carries the thinking above. It scrolls smoothly and only shows each card once the screen settles.
- Mark handled: the button turns into a lime check, the card flashes, a line says the volunteer will see it, then the card slides into Handled. If no staff name is set, it asks for one right in the card.
- Bug: the staff page crashed on every refresh after the first, so Mark handled and Approve looked like they did nothing. Fixed.
- Staff headline reads as a sentence.
